Personal Health Data Viewer

This is a personal, non-commercial project intended to let its owner securely view their own health information through read-only, standards-based FHIR APIs.

Privacy: This site does not collect, sell, or share health information. Any future application will use OAuth authorization and will access only the data authorized by its owner.

Purpose

The project is being developed to aggregate and review personal clinical records, including laboratory results, medications, visits, and related health information. It is not a diagnostic, treatment, emergency, or medical-device service.

Contact

For questions about this personal integration, contact the project owner through the associated GitHub repository.
